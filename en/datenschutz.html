2. In short

M-Projekt is an app for tasks, boards and calendars. We run no servers of our own and use no trackers and no analytics tools. Your tasks, projects and comments are stored on your device and in your iCloud account with Apple. We have no access to them.

If you set up an external AI provider or your own transcription server, the app can send content directly to the selected provider with your consent. If you download a local speech model, the download service receives technical connection data.

4. Data the app processes

The app processes the following data solely to provide its features:

  • Tasks and subtasks (title, notes, due date, duration, priority, recurrence, tags, status)
  • Projects, boards, templates and your settings
  • Task comments, including @mentions
  • Task images, file attachments and person avatars
  • People in your projects: name, avatar if any, and for invited members the iCloud participant details provided by Apple; for external contributors without iCloud that you add yourself, name and note
  • Email addresses you enter to invite people to shared projects (invitation handled by Apple)
  • Events from the calendars you select; reminders from Apple Reminders if you import or link them

If you enter data about other people, such as contributors' names, you usually do so for private purposes. Please only add what collaboration requires.

Images and file attachments

Images, file attachments and avatars are stored on your device and synced via CloudKit. Members can see them in shared projects. For readable images, including pictures attached as files or restored from a backup, the app removes sensitive metadata such as GPS location, device serial numbers and personal details. If it detects sensitive metadata and cannot remove it, the app rejects the image. Technical details such as orientation may be retained. Other file types, such as PDFs, are kept as supplied.

Backup

Backup exports (.kanbanbackup) are password-protected and may include images and file attachments. New exports use AES-GCM and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 with 600,000 iterations. To import a backup, you need its password. Keep the file and password safe. You choose where to save the file. The app also creates encrypted local snapshots for recovery using a key in the local keychain. Local app data and snapshots may be included in a device backup if you use that system feature.

Spotlight, Siri, widgets and notifications

  • Spotlight: Task and project titles are added to your device's local search index so you can find them in iOS search.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: If you use M-Projekt actions with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes your voice request according to your Siri settings. M-Projekt only receives the result, for example the title of a new task.
  • Widgets: For widgets, the app stores a selection of your tasks and events, plus display information, in local storage shared between the app and its widgets (App Group).
  • Notifications: If you allow notifications, the app schedules task reminders and briefings as local notifications on your device. Apple's push service only delivers the signal that something changed in iCloud.

What the app does not collect

  • No analytics or tracking data
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No proprietary accounts or login credentials
  • No data on servers of our own, as we run none

Apple frameworks used

  • CloudKit: storing and syncing your data in your iCloud account, sharing
  • EventKit: access to calendars and reminders
  • StoreKit: in-app purchases
  • Speech: on-device speech recognition
  • FoundationModels: AI assistant (Apple Intelligence on device and Apple Private Cloud Compute)
  • MetricKit: crash and hang reports from iOS, evaluated locally
  • App Intents, Core Spotlight, WidgetKit, UserNotifications

Children

The App Store age rating describes the app's content. It does not define a target audience. M-Projekt contains no advertising and does not record usage behaviour. Children under 16 can consent to a transfer to third-party AI or transcription services only with parental consent (Art. 8 GDPR). These services often have their own age limits.

5. iCloud and sync

Your data in CloudKit

Tasks, projects, boards, comments, people, images, attachments, templates, tags and settings are stored on your device. When iCloud sync is active, the app syncs them through Apple’s CloudKit.

  • Private database: this stores your own project data. Other users cannot access projects you haven’t shared.
  • Shared projects: the project is stored in its owner’s private CloudKit database. Other members access it through their shared CloudKit database. This includes comments, images, attachments and permissions.

Apple encrypts this data in transit and at rest. Advanced Data Protection for iCloud can end-to-end encrypt designated CloudKit fields and CloudKit assets. The protection also depends on how the data is shared. We cannot view your project content. For details, see Apple’s iCloud data security overview.

You can use your own projects and tasks locally. Syncing across devices and collaborating on shared projects require an iCloud account and active iCloud sync.

Calendar display (iCloud key-value store)

Which projects and calendars the calendar shows is synced between your devices via Apple's iCloud key-value store. Only internal identifiers (IDs) of projects and calendars plus display toggles are stored, no content. The data resides in your iCloud account. Without iCloud it stays on the device only.

API key

A stored AI API key is synced end-to-end encrypted to your other devices via iCloud Keychain (see "AI features").

Apple as recipient

Apple provides iCloud services under your agreement with Apple and is responsible for them itself (in the EU: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland). Apple may also process data in the USA. Apple Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (European Commission adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR). More: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Calendar and Reminders

Calendars (EventKit)

If you allow calendar access, the app reads events from the calendars you select and shows them in Today, Week and Calendar. At your request it can create, edit or delete events where the calendar allows it. Access is optional and can be revoked at any time in system settings. Section 7 explains when event information may be sent to your own AI API.

Apple Reminders (EventKit)

If you allow access, the app can import reminders from Apple Reminders and link them to tasks. Imported tasks are stored in M-Projekt (see section 5). For linked reminders, the app can update details such as completion status in Apple Reminders. The original reminders remain in Apple Reminders. The app does not access your address book. Project participants come from your entries and iCloud sharing.

7. AI features

The AI features are optional. They include the AI assistant, suggestions and briefings, and “Write with AI” for notes. Depending on the feature, you can review and apply a suggestion or ask the assistant to make a change in the app. Automatic filing through your own API can be enabled separately. AI results can be wrong. Check them before using them. The app labels AI-generated results, for example note drafts.

Two modes

  • Apple (Apple Intelligence): The app uses Apple’s Foundation Models for on-device processing. This requires a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled. Input stays on your device when processed this way. For larger requests the app uses Apple’s Private Cloud Compute when available: the request is then processed on Apple servers and, according to Apple, is not stored or made accessible to Apple. If Private Cloud Compute is unavailable or its quota is used up, the app processes on the device.
  • Own API (provider of your choice): You select a provider or configure your own endpoint. An external service requires the matching API key and your consent. A local endpoint you operate yourself can work without a key.

Explicit consent before the first transfer

Before the app first sends content to a third-party AI provider, it shows a consent dialog. The dialog identifies the address, categories of data and purpose. It also explains that the provider may process data outside the EU and links to this privacy policy. If you decline, the app does not send the content.

  • Consent applies per provider address (scheme, host, port) and purpose. Automatic filing through your own API prompts for a separate confirmation. If you switch providers, the app asks again.
  • If the consent text changes materially, the app asks again.
  • Consent is stored only on the respective device and is not synced via iCloud. On another device you will be asked again, even if your AI settings were synced there.
  • You can revoke consent at any time in the AI settings (Art. 7(3) GDPR). After that the app sends nothing more to that provider until you consent again. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
  • Local servers on your own network (e.g. LM Studio, Ollama) do not count as third-party providers. The app does not ask for consent for them.
  • The connection test and loading the model list in the AI settings send no content from the app, only a fixed test text or a bundled test recording and your key for that provider. The app does not ask for consent for these.

Provider choice, responsibility and third countries

The available presets are OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Mistral, Google Gemini, Groq, Fireworks, Together, xAI (Grok), Cerebras, Mammouth, LM Studio, Ollama (local) and Ollama Cloud. You can enter other OpenAI-compatible endpoints under “Custom”. An earlier DeepSeek setting may be retained as a custom endpoint.

You choose the provider yourself and enter into the usage relationship directly with them. The provider processes the data under its own responsibility, privacy policy and terms. Please read them before using "Own API" mode. We do not receive the transmitted data.

Many providers are based or process data outside the EU, especially in the USA. Depending on the provider:

  • Providers with an EU contracting entity or EU standard contractual clauses: OpenAI contracts with EEA customers through OpenAI Ireland Ltd. and protects transfers outside the EEA with standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).
  • Providers without an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards: This may include providers in countries for which the EU has not found an equivalent level of data protection. There, authorities may be able to access data without you having effective legal remedies. Where the conditions under Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR are met, a transfer may rely on your explicit consent after you have been informed of the risks.

You can check whether your provider is certified under the Data Privacy Framework at https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list.

What is transmitted in "Own API" mode

Assistant requests send your input, the model instructions, the history of that conversation and, depending on the feature, a selection of data from the chosen scope. The app also stores your chat history on your device and keeps it out of CloudKit sync. The context may include:

  • Project names with internal ID plus counts of open tasks and boards (up to 50 projects)
  • Board names with internal ID, frequent tags and sample task titles (up to 20 boards)
  • Titles of open tasks (up to 20 by default)
  • Up to 30 tasks with title, internal ID, board and project name, a notes excerpt (up to 140 characters), tags and the names of assigned people
  • Names and internal IDs of people from your projects and shared projects (up to 30)
  • Frequently used tags
  • In the weekly view additionally: titles and identifiers of events from the selected calendars (up to 5 per day) and titles of planned tasks
  • Your own chat input
  • Results of the tools the assistant calls during a request, such as tasks found or created with their due dates. If you have allowed the assistant to use your calendar, also calendar names and event titles and times. For calendars with the same name, the account name may be used to distinguish them, for example an email address.
  • When summarising a task, any comments with their authors’ names and attachment file names
  • Depending on the feature, your device’s date, time and time zone

For suggestions and briefings, the app sends counts and selected details from tasks and events, such as shortened titles, times, due dates and priorities. The briefing context excludes content from shared projects, notes, comments and attachments. For “Write with AI”, the app sends the task and subtask titles, project and board names, due date and existing note text. If you separately enable automatic filing with your own API, the app sends the title and note of each new task from Quick Capture. Context can include project, board and person names, a few open task titles, events for that day if you have allowed calendar access, and the date, time and time zone.

AI requests contain text, which may include file names. Image data, avatars and file contents are excluded from these requests.

Speech input

  • Apple dictation (default): Speech recognition runs on your device through Apple's Speech framework (SpeechTranscriber, with SFSpeechRecognizer as a fallback, also restricted to on-device recognition). iOS downloads the required language model from Apple when needed. The recording itself does not leave your device. Neither we nor your AI provider receive any audio.
  • Local speech models (optional): Parakeet TDT v3 and Whisper Large V3 Turbo process the recording on your device. If you download a model, the app fetches its files from Hugging Face. Hugging Face may process connection data in the United States. This involves technical connection data such as your IP address. The app sends no recordings or transcripts for the download. For information about processing and retention by the download service, see the Hugging Face privacy policy.
  • Own server (optional): If you switch speech input to your own transcription server, the app sends your recording as a WAV file to the configured transcription endpoint, along with the model name, a language code where applicable, and the API key over HTTPS if you use one. This also applies when the AI assistant runs in "Apple" mode. The app points this out in Settings. If the server is not on your own network, the app asks for your explicit consent before the first upload. This consent is separate from the one for the AI assistant, applies per server address and per device, and can be revoked at any time. Without consent the device recognizes the recording itself. You can store a separate key for the server. If none is set, the chat provider's key is used only when both services run at the same address. If a required key is missing or the upload fails, the app keeps the recording temporarily. You can retry or process it on your device. The server may already have received the recording during a failed attempt. For third countries, the information under "Provider choice" applies.

API key

Your API key is stored in your device's keychain and can sync end-to-end encrypted across your devices when settings sync and iCloud Keychain are enabled. It is used solely to authenticate with the selected provider and is never transmitted to us. The key is bound to the provider address you saved it for: if you switch providers, it is not sent to the new provider. Settings then show that a key is missing for that provider.

A key is never sent over unencrypted HTTP to servers on the internet. For a local AI chat server using HTTP, you can explicitly allow the key for that address. The app sends transcription API keys only over HTTPS. Any content or chat key sent over HTTP is unencrypted and may be read on the network.

Turning it off

In Settings you can switch AI requests to Apple mode, withdraw consent for a provider or delete its stored key. Speech input and its consent are managed separately in Settings. Contact the provider directly about data already sent.

8. Sharing with others

When you share a project, you invite people through Apple's iCloud sharing, for example by email address or link. Apple handles the invitation and may display the project name and an app icon in the sharing preview. We do not receive or store these details.

CloudKit makes shared projects, including tasks, comments, images, attachments, people and permissions, available to authorised participants. They can also see your participant name as shown by Apple. Depending on the sharing settings, people with an invitation link can join. If someone leaves a project, their comments remain visible to the others.

9. Diagnostic data

The app keeps a size-limited diagnostic log on your device. Older entries are overwritten and you can turn off recording. The app logs technical information from crash and hang reports provided by iOS through MetricKit. The app does not send the log automatically. You can share it yourself using the share sheet in the diagnostics view. The default export hides content marked as personal, such as names and task titles. You can choose to include it in the export. If you send us the log, we use it only to investigate the reported problem and delete it afterwards (see section 13).

If the app sets aside damaged or unreadable local data, you can also create and share a support export yourself. The ZIP file is unencrypted and may contain local task and project data from the latest snapshot and any files set aside. If you send it to us, the same retention period applies as for the diagnostic log.

12. Your rights

  • restriction of processing (Art. 18),
  • data portability (Art. 20),

13. Retention and deletion

  • Data in the app and in iCloud: kept until you delete it in the app. To remove local data, delete the app. To delete M-Projekt's CloudKit data, go to iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › M-Projekt (the wording may differ by iOS version).
  • Backups: exported files stay where you saved them until you delete them. The app keeps up to six automatic local snapshots and removes older ones. Snapshots taken before an account switch are kept until you delete them.
  • Shared projects: if you delete a project you own, it is removed for all members. If you leave someone else's project, it disappears from your devices.
  • Reminders and events: remain in Apple Reminders and Calendar. Remove them there.
  • API key: delete it in the AI settings. It is then also removed from iCloud Keychain.
  • Consents for third-party AI/transcription: stored locally on the device until you revoke them or delete the app.
  • Diagnostics and troubleshooting: the local log is overwritten on a rolling basis. Diagnostic logs and support exports you send us are deleted once the bug is fixed, at the latest after 12 months.
  • Emails: deleted once your request is settled, at the latest after 12 months, unless statutory retention obligations apply (e.g. 6 years for business letters under § 257 German Commercial Code / § 147 German Fiscal Code).
  • At AI or transcription providers: according to their rules.

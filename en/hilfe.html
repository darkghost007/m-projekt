Sync

How does M-Projekt sync between my devices?

M-Projekt uses iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account on your devices and check that sync is active in the app under Settings › iCloud & Sync.

Does the app work offline?

You can keep working offline with projects and tasks that are already loaded. If iCloud sync is enabled, changes sync when your device is back online.

The sync indicator shows an error. What now?

Tap the sync indicator to see the status. Once you are back online, you can retry the sync under Settings › iCloud & Sync.

Sharing

How do I share a project?

Open the project and tap the people icon at the top, or “…” › Members. From there you invite people or manage sharing. Sharing your own projects is part of Premium; invitees don’t need Premium.

How do I change roles or remove someone?

In the sharing sheet, tap the person: choose “Read only” or “Read and write”, or “Remove from project”. Only the project owner can do this.

How do I leave a shared project?

People icon in the project header › Sharing › “Leave project”. The project disappears from your devices; your comments stay visible to the others.

AI setup

Which AI does M-Projekt use?

You can use Apple Intelligence – on your device and, when available, through Apple’s Private Cloud Compute. Alternatively you can use your own API key or a local server on your home network. See the step-by-step guide under AI setup.

How do I apply AI suggestions?

Depending on the feature, you can review and apply suggestions or ask the assistant to make a change directly. You can enable automatic filing with your own API separately in Settings.

Premium

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in M-Projekt and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the Apple Account you purchased with.

How does Premium work?

Premium unlocks with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription or trial.

Export

How do I export or import a backup?

Open Settings › iCloud & Sync. Use “Export backup” to save a password-protected file. To restore it, choose “Import backup”, select the file and enter its password.

How do I share a diagnostics log with support?

Open Settings › iCloud & Sync › Diagnostics Log. Tap “Share log” to attach it to your support email. You can leave “Include names and content” off.
